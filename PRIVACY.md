# 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

最后更新：2026-10-26

## 中文

Suzuran Launcher（PCL 铃兰修改版）不会收集、上传或出售任何个人信息。

- 启动器不要求注册账号，也不运营任何自建服务器。
- 使用微软正版登录时，认证在微软官方页面完成，启动器不会接触你的账号密码。
- 登录成功后获得的访问令牌与刷新令牌仅保存在你本机的 PCL 配置目录中，仅用于维持登录状态，不会发送给开发者或任何第三方。
- 启动器日志保存在本机 PCL 目录，不会自动上传；如需反馈问题，请自行检查内容后再手动提供。
- 启动器不会读取与游戏无关的个人文件。
- 更换启动页插画、导入整合包等操作均只在本机进行。

## English

Suzuran Launcher does not collect, upload or sell any personal information.

- No account registration is required, and the launcher operates no backend service of its own.
- Microsoft sign-in is performed on Microsoft's own pages; the launcher never sees the user's password.
- Access and refresh tokens are stored only in the local PCL configuration directory on the user's device and are used solely to keep the user signed in. They are never sent to the developer or to third parties.
- Log files are written locally and are never uploaded automatically.
- The launcher does not read personal files unrelated to the game.
- Changing the startup illustration or importing modpacks happens entirely on the local device.
